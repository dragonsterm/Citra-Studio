    Workspace guide

    Each result widget processes the same source, so you can compare different methods or settings side by side.

    Choose a source
    Open or drop a photo, start the webcam, or load the sample.
    Add methods
    Click a method or drag it into the workspace. Hold Ctrl while clicking to select several methods, then drag them together or choose Add.
    Arrange results
    Grab the body or title of a tile to move it. Pin a tile to keep it in the top section. Drag its bottom corner to resize.
    Inspect and save
    Select a tile for its histogram, formula and settings. Save the selected image with the download button.
    Compare live telemetry
    Open the bottom dock waveform to compare histograms. Drag cards to reorder them, or click a chart to lock the same intensity bin across all cards.
    Ctrl+O Open photo Ctrl+S Save image / Search methods
